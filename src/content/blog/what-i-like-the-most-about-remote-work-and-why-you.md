## Why I Love Remote Work (And Why You Might Too)

Remote work has come a long way from being a niche perk to a widely adopted way of life. While the concept of telecommuting dates back to the 1970s, it wasn’t until the global pandemic in 2020 that it truly became mainstream (Nilles, 1994). By then, companies had no choice but to embrace flexibility.

As we adapted, many of us discovered unexpected benefits and for me, remote work quickly became more than just a necessity; it became a lifestyle I genuinely enjoy. With powerful digital tools and changing work cultures, more people are choosing flexibility, and I think you should consider it too.

## Why I Love Remote Work

### 1. More Time for My Mornings

One of the best parts of remote work is the ability to start my day without rushing. No more sitting in traffic or running to catch the bus. Instead, I can go for a walk, get a quick workout in, play with my pets, or do some light cleaning before sitting down to work. I also get to enjoy home-cooked meals, which not only helps me eat healthier but also saves me a lot of money compared to eating out every day.

### 2. Freedom to Work from Anywhere

Remote work gives me the freedom to switch up my environment. Some days, I’ll work from a cozy coffee shop; other days, I’ll set up near the beach and enjoy the view while tackling my to-do list. Having this variety helps keep me inspired and energized. It’s a small luxury that makes a big difference in how I experience my workdays.

### 3. A Workspace That’s Uniquely Mine

I love having control over my workspace. I’ve personalized my home office with things that make me feel motivated and focused: Rubik’s cubes, photos of friends and family, decorative lights, and sticky notes with little affirmations. It’s a space that reflects who I am and helps me feel at home, even while I’m getting work done. And let’s face it, some people will think having puzzles is like playing with a toy at work, but it actually helps me focus.

## Why You Should Try It Too

If you’re craving a more flexible schedule, the ability to work from anywhere, or opportunities to collaborate with companies beyond your geographic area, remote work might be exactly what you need. You’ll save on commuting costs, eat better, and get to design a space that works for you. Remote work isn’t just a trend, it’s a way to take ownership of your time and energy.

Give it a shot. Your ideal workday might be more within reach than you think.
